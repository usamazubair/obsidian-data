Synthesized advanced LinkedIn search tactics for freelance recruitment opportunities

Three different searches, three different purposes. The posts search is the one that actually produces clients — most people skip it and grind connections instead.

### 1. Posts search — highest intent, do this daily

Search the string, then switch the tab to **Posts** and set **Date posted → Past 24 hours** or **Past week**. These are people saying out loud that they need you.

```
("looking for a developer" OR "need a developer" OR "hiring a developer") AND (freelance OR contract OR "part time")
```

```
("looking for" OR "need") AND ("full stack developer" OR "react developer" OR "node developer")
```

```
("MVP" OR "prototype") AND ("looking for a developer" OR "need a technical" OR "no technical co-founder")
```

```
("need help with" OR "looking for someone") AND (dashboard OR "internal tool" OR "custom software")
```

```
("react developer" OR "next.js developer") AND (remote OR freelance) AND (hiring OR "reach out")
```

Set **Sort by → Latest**. On a fresh post you're one of the first three comments; on a three-day-old post you're the fortieth.

### 2. People search — build your target list

Switch tab to **People**, then filter.

```
(founder OR "co-founder" OR CEO OR CTO) AND (SaaS OR startup) AND ("looking for" OR building)
```

```
("head of operations" OR "operations director" OR COO) AND ("field sales" OR distribution OR logistics)
```

```
(founder OR CEO) AND ("field sales" OR "direct sales" OR "sales agency")
```
